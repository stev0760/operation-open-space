# core-0001: rock hits, damage lamps and repair, the player's death, and leaving a wave through the gate

- **Date:** 2026-11-01. **Host:** Wine 11.0 old WoW64 with the ExtractIcon patch (`~/opt/wine-11.0-oldwow-patched`, the `oracle/env.sh` default), Xvfb 1024×768×24, PulseAudio with `PULSE_SINK` on a null sink.
- **Prefix:** `work/wine/m4s1-oracle` (`ORACLE_ID=80`, display `:190`), made fresh for each run by `scenario_prefix` of `oracle/scenarios/common.sh`: game layout, `--with-ini`, fixture `basic` as `D:`, no `Z:`. Unregistered. `INI_SET="SoundSystem=1 MixChannels=1"`, so every sound shows in the trace as a `SNDPLAYSOUND` of its slot, as in [sound-0001](sound-0001.md).
- **Trace:** relay profile `wave` (`oracle/trace.sh`).
- **Memory:** `isprobe watch` (`oracle/probe/isprobe.c`) polled INSPACE.EXE DGROUP every 50 ms. `DGROUP:0x70ae` (16 bytes) covers words +0x1e to +0x2c of ship record 0, the player ([structs.md](../../docs/re/structs.md#ship-records-8--0x15a-bytes-at-dgroup0x7090)). `DGROUP:0x7170` (16 bytes) covers bytes +0xe0 to +0xef. The watch logs are `watch-systems.txt` and `watch-alive.txt`, with wall-clock times as in `steps.txt`. [DESIGN]
- **Scripts** (not in the repository): `work/oracle/core-0001/run.sh` reaches wave 1 as `oracle/scenarios/wave.sh` does, then runs stages from `funcs.sh`. `lib.sh` keeps driving a game left running (`KEEP=1`). `pilot.py` is a copy of `oracle/scenarios/autopilot.py` that steers at the nearest rock (grey-brown blobs, or red ones for hot rocks) or at the gate beam (pixels #00FFFF), and reads the four lamp pixels at x 600, y 715 + 14k.
- **Reproduce:** `ORACLE_ID=80 WINEPREFIX=$PWD/work/wine/m4s1-oracle PULSE_SINK=<null sink> KEEP=1 STAGES="hud hit" INI_SET="SoundSystem=1 MixChannels=1" work/oracle/core-0001/run.sh work/oracle/core-0001/hit`. Then source `lib.sh` with `OUT` set to the run directory, start the watchers, and call `ram_close`, `heat_ram`, `flail` or `flail_to_death` and `shooter`, as logged in `steps.txt`. The sound lists come from `work/oracle/sound-0001-tools/sound_calls.py RUNDIR` (`sound_calls.out` in each run).
- **Bulk output (gitignored):** `work/oracle/core-0001/`.
  - `hit/`: the first game. It holds hits 1–5, the shoot-and-ram cycles `d1`–`d6`, the thrust-and-turn loop `f1`, and the unplanned exit through the gate at 0.15 s intervals (`death-c*.png`, named before the exit happened). Then the debriefing and Navigation. 726 screenshots, HUD crops `*-hud.png`, a 230 MB trace.
  - `death/`: the second game. Wave 1 ends at the gate without input, wave 2 ends at the gate 3 s after its start, and in wave 3 the ship dies. Shots of wave 3 to the death are `death-c*.png` (0.15 s), shots after the MessageBox are `after-c*.png` (0.44 s). 3038 screenshots.
  - `gate/`: a planned exit that failed, because the beam was not on screen at the wave start (see Not covered).
- **Times:** t is seconds from the first trace line of the run. The `hit` run's wave starts at t = 44.2 and the `death` run's wave 1 at t = 44.1. Wall-clock times convert with the F12 MessageBox (`hit`) and the history MessageBox (`death`) to within 0.1 s.
