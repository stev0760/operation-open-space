# core-0002: icon capture, the gun, the missiles and the HUD pictures

- **Date:** 2026-11-02. **Host:** Wine 11.0 old WoW64 with the ExtractIcon patch (`~/opt/wine-11.0-oldwow-patched`, the `oracle/env.sh` default), Xvfb 1024×768×24, PulseAudio with `PULSE_SINK` on a null sink.
- **Prefix:** `work/wine/m4s2-oracle` (`ORACLE_ID=88`, display `:198`), made fresh for each run by `scenario_prefix` of `oracle/scenarios/common.sh`: shipdisk layout, `--with-ini`, fixture `basic` as `D:`, no `Z:`. Unregistered, TRANSFORMER. `INI_SET="SoundSystem=1 MixChannels=1"`, so every sound shows in the trace as a `SNDPLAYSOUND` of its slot, as in [core-0001](core-0001.md).
- **Trace:** relay profile `wave` (`oracle/trace.sh`).
- **Memory:** `isprobe watch` (`oracle/probe/isprobe.c`) polled INSPACE.EXE DGROUP every 50 ms from 2 s after the wave start: the score DGROUP:0x1e58 (dword), the rating DGROUP:0x5078 (dword), the capture value DGROUP:0x9604, DGROUP:0x9432, the pool-6 count DGROUP:0x9412, and the player's ship bytes +0xa0 … +0xd3 (DGROUP:0x7130, 0x34 bytes: +0xae ammo, +0xb8 weapons, +0xcb, +0xcc, +0xce `resources`, +0xd0 destroyed, +0xd2 captured). The gun runs add bytes +0x70 … +0x7f of each shot slot (DGROUP:0x6108 + 0x94k), the missile run the 0x1c-byte missile entries 0 … 5 at DGROUP:0x66e0 + 0x1c·m. `isprobe` prints at most 64 bytes of a span, so the slots and entries are watched one by one. The logs are `watch-<name>.txt` in each run directory, with wall-clock times as in `steps.txt`. [DESIGN]
- **Scripts** (not in the repository): `work/oracle/core-0002/run.sh` reaches wave 1 as `oracle/scenarios/wave.sh` does, presses F12 → No (the full status display), starts the watchers and runs `part_$PART` from `funcs.sh`. `pilot.py` is core-0001's helper plus the icon target of `oracle/scenarios/autopilot.py` (icon faces on screen, else radar dots) and a `capture` loop that thrusts without braking while the heading error is below 12°. `pdiff.py`, `dots.py`, `white.py` and `msgspan.py` read the watch logs and the screenshots.
- **Reproduce:** `ORACLE_ID=88 WINEPREFIX=$PWD/work/wine/m4s2-oracle PULSE_SINK=<null sink> INI_SET="SoundSystem=1 MixChannels=1" PART=<part> work/oracle/core-0002/run.sh work/oracle/core-0002/<run>`, with `PART` one of `capture`, `gun`, `gun2`, `missile`, `shatter`. The sound lists come from `work/oracle/sound-0001-tools/sound_calls.py RUNDIR` (`sound_calls.out` in each run).

  - `capture/` and `capture-b/`: the capture part, screenshots every 0.2 s (`capN-c*.png`) from the start of each approach to 10 s after the score change, then every 0.5 s for 8 s (`capN-late-c*.png`). `capture/` captured once (the first approach braked too often to arrive), `capture-b/` twice.
  - `gun/` (0.3 s Space presses at 200–300 px) and `gun2/` (1.2 s presses after an approach to about 110 px), screenshots every 0.1 s (`rock-c*`, `rock2-c*`, `icon-c*`).
  - `missile/`: one Z, then three Z 2 s apart, then Tab twice; screenshots every 0.1 s (`mis1-c*`, `mis2-c*`).
  - `shatter/`: 1.0 s presses at an icon from about 280 px, stopped by hand once no icon was left.
  - `shatter-b/`: the same with 1.5 s presses from about 240 px, 14 cycles; three icon hits (0x12) and no shatter.
  - `picture/`: the HUD crops of [Pictures](#pictures).

The F12 MessageBox ties t to the wall clock to within 0.1 s: t = 46.270 at 12:37:49.12 (`capture`), 46.233 at 12:50:23.50 (`capture-b`), 46.228 at 12:42:46.06 (`gun`), 46.240 at 12:52:21.72 (`gun2`), 46.232 at 13:00:21.43 (`missile`) and 46.238 at 13:03:43.66 (`shatter`). ## Gun
- **Fire rate.** A held Space fires one shot every 0.134–0.146 s (5 frames): a 0.3 s press gives 2 or 3 shots, 1.2 s 8 or 9. `gun` fired 149 shots, and the trace holds 149 `SNDPLAYSOUND`s of slot 0x03 (ONE_SHOT) after the wave start, one per shot, each after a pair of `SNDPLAYSOUND(NULL)` stop calls 0–15 ms earlier. No 0x04 plays: the TRANSFORMER's slot barrel count is 1. [ORACLE core-0002]
- **Ship words per shot.** Dword +0xa0 takes the frame stamp at every shot. Byte +0xcc goes 0 → 7 at the first shot of a game and stays 7. Each shot adds 1 to DGROUP:0x9432 (`gun2` 12:52:36.23 … 37.28: 0x57 → 0x5f for 9 shots). [ORACLE core-0002]
- **Slot words** (`gun2/watch-slot0.txt`, as words +0x70 … +0x7e): a new shot reads velocity (18, −45), lifetime +0x74 = 102, barrels +0x78 = 1, owner +0x7a = 14, +0x7c = 0, hits +0x7e = 0. The age +0x76 rises by 5 per frame (+1 per sub-step), so a shot lives about 20 frames, 0.55 s, and the age turns −1 (0xffff) after 0x5f … 0x64. With 1.2 s presses slots 0 … 4 take shots in turn; 5 … 8 stay unused. Before the first shot slot 0 reads lifetime 50 and age −1. [ORACLE core-0002]
- **Ricochet.** A shot that hits a rock keeps flying: in the same poll its velocity changes (18, −45) → (43, 14) and its hit count +0x7e becomes 1, and it expires at the lifetime (12:52:36.426 … 36.777). Each hit plays one 0x11 (RICOCHET), for example eight in 1 s at t = 60.918 … 61.921 in `gun2`, one per shot of a 1.2 s press. [ORACLE core-0002]
- **The shot's picture** (`dots.py` on `gun/rock-c0099` … `c0102`): each shot is a trail of five 2 × 2 px dots, 2 px apart in x and 5–6 px in y along its path. The colour steps with age: a new trail is #FFFF00 and the oldest dots seen are #691900; #DCD700, #C88C00, #AF6400, #FF8000 and #874100 appear between them, and the order of the middle steps was not resolved. [ORACLE core-0002]
- **Rock heat.** A big rock hit by several shots turns into a dithered mix of red-orange and its grey (`gun2/rock-c0336` … `c0340`), and back towards grey over the next seconds. [ORACLE core-0002]
- **No kill by shots.** In `gun` (36 presses of 0.3 s) and `gun2` (four 1.2 s presses at 50–140 px) no rock broke under fire and the score did not move. [ORACLE core-0002]
- **Kills by ramming a heated rock.** Every score step of 900 in these runs (`gun2` 12:53:19.75 and 12:57:15.25, `shatter` 13:04:25.23 and 13:08:32.76) comes with 0x07, 0x08 and 0x45 (KABOOM) in the same 10 ms: the ship rams a rock that the gun had heated, and the rock splits into three small orange rocks (`gun2/rock-c0341` … `c0343`).
