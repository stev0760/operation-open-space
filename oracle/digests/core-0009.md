## What every press does (all weapons)
- **A held Z fires at its release.** In every weapon that fired, the held Z (down 1.5 s) changed the fuel, pool or sound words within 0.00–0.02 s of its **release**, never at its press; a Z pressed and released at once changes them 0.00–0.03 s after the press (`xdotool key`). So the weapon fires on the key-up. The hooks add a second rule (weapons 2 and 15). [ORACLE core-0009]
- **Refusals.** The presses with no launch evidence (no sound, fuel, count or pool change) came in two kinds: (a) 0.08–0.41 s after a hit sound on the ship (0x08, 0x09 or 0x0e): MINES Z2 (0.37 s), DUMB MISSILES Z3 (0.08 s; Z1 too, after a hit in the lead-in), RADAR-GUIDED Z2 (0.41 s), HEATSEEKER Z2 (0.27 s), RING OF DEATH Z2 (0.19 s); launches that came 0.42, 0.45, 0.49 and 0.55 s after a hit sound went ahead (FIRE DEMONS Z2, SPUD MISSILES Z4, PROTECTIVE PELLETS Z4, TARGETTING MISSILES `targetting2` Z4); the hit sounds' times are the sounds', not the impacts'; (b) with no hit at all: every press of WILDCARD DEFENSE, five of the six of LIGHTNING in `lightning2` and `lightning` Z2, Z4, Z5, and HANDCUFFS' first press. core-0006's rule (no launch within 0.4 s of a hit) holds here. [ORACLE core-0009]
- **Count and cost.** Weapons 1 to 5, 13, 2 and 15 (the "FUEL - …" and the hook rows) cost fuel and leave the count byte +0xaf at 20; every other weapon costs one count per launch and no fuel: after six launches the display reads 14 (batteries, doughnuts, stars, targetting, death heads, pellets, beer, spud), after five 15 (radar, heatseeker, mines, firedemons). [ORACLE core-0009]
- **Sounds** (names from `re/artifacts/sounds.tsv`): 0x02 ZOOM, 0x03 ONE_SHOT, 0x06 CLUNK, 0x07 BOUNCE, 0x08 SHORT, 0x09 SMASH, 0x0e ORCA, 0x18 NEW_DEF, 0x19 FIRE1, 0x1b FIREBALL, 0x1c MISSILE, 0x1d SLING, 0x1e FUEL, 0x1f SHATTER, 0x22 BOLT, 0x23 CAPTURED, 0x4e WHISTLE. 0x45 (KABOOM) repeats every 75 ms while the ship thrusts and is left out of every table. [BYTES SND_FX.DLL] [DESIGN]
- **Repeated sounds.** `timeline.txt` condenses the repeats of one sound into one line, and the press tables take its first time, so a 0x02 of weapons 9 and 18 – 21 listed at +0.01 s is the first of 2 – 8 plays: the trace (MMSYSTEM.2 SNDPLAYSOUND of the slot-0x02 selector) plays 0x02 at the launch and at each spin wrap up to about 0.9 s.
