## The screen
- **The full status display is drawn during play:** the circuit-board border on all four sides, the bevelled frame around the play area, SCORE (`430 100`) at the lower left, the target display ("CURRENT TARGET (MANUAL)", an icon, "FUZZY 19 / HEART OF GOLD / FUZZY ONES / ALLY +37"), the Fuel gauge, the radar, the scrollbar, the clock (`0:28`) with its red button, the damage panel (Engine, Gun, Thrust, Cloak, each with a green lamp) and the weapon panel ("WEAPON SYSTEM", "FIRE DEMONS", with a weapon picture: a spiked ball and a flame). The user confirms it was already there at the wave start, before any key was pressed, and that a setting can turn it off ([MANUAL Display Options]), which the user never does. So the bare display that the Wine oracle shows at the wave start (wave-0001 shots 05–12) is not what the game shows on Windows, and the port draws the full display from the first frame of the wave ([ADR 0013](../../docs/decisions/0013-wave-drawing-and-input.md)). [ORACLE win98-0001]
- **The icon column** at the right edge holds the wave's icons in colour (seven visible), with a yellow warning triangle among them. [ORACLE win98-0001]
- **The Ship Watch window** (the W key, [MANUAL Dealing With Other Ships]) at the top left, titled "HEART OF GOLD - FUZZY 19" with a Windows 98 caption bar and close box, shows the ship's icon and two lines: "FUZZY ONES - ALLY +37" and "I'M ATTACKING THE NEMESIS. I WARNED HIM ENOUGH TIMES." The user opened it before the photograph to show the Windows 98 window frame. [ORACLE win98-0001]
