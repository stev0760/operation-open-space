## How the game picks the GetSystemPaletteEntries range
- The code at ISPACE.DLL seg5:0x3644–0x3707 reads `GetDeviceCaps` SIZEPALETTE (stored at ISPACE.DLL DGROUP:0x1e98), NUMRESERVED, BITSPIXEL and PLANES. [BYTES ISPACE.DLL seg5:0x3644]
  - SIZEPALETTE 0 becomes 16, and NUMRESERVED 0 becomes 16.
  - With max(PLANES, BITSPIXEL) = 4 it makes one call, `(hdc, 0, 16)`.
  - Otherwise it calls `(hdc, 0, NUMRESERVED/2)` and `(hdc, SIZEPALETTE − NUMRESERVED/2, NUMRESERVED/2)`.
- The traced arguments match: SIZEPALETTE 256 and NUMRESERVED 20 give `(0, 10)` and `(246, 10)` at depth 8. SIZEPALETTE 0 (taken as 16) gives `(6, 10)` at depth 24. [ORACLE depth8-0002]
